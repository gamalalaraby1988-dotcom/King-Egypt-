# Royal Ship Entrance — Approved Visual Concept

**Status:** User-approved visual direction; proposal only, not implemented in the game.

## Approved appearance and arrangement
- Keep the **Five Civilizations Ship** stationary and moored at its approved location.
- Connect the shore-side royal road to the ship approach with one continuous, broad royal bridge.
- The bridge must support **two-way vehicle traffic**. Retain protected pedestrian paths along both sides, separated from vehicles by rails or equivalent barriers.
- Place the **glowing circular interaction pad directly in front of the ship's main entrance gate**, at the ship end of the bridge. It must not sit halfway along the bridge.
- When a player enters the circle, show the interaction prompt; after the player presses it and confirms **Enter Ship**, invoke the previously approved ship-entry/transition flow to the ship's main hall.
- Preserve the approved Five Civilizations Ship identity: ivory-white and gold, Egyptian/civilization motifs, grand main hall and five civilization wings. Do not redesign it as an ordinary yacht.

## Traffic and pedestrian safety
- The earlier 13 m bridge width is a **preliminary planning dimension**, not a final engineering approval: 8.5 m two-way vehicle carriageway plus 2.25 m protected pedestrian path on each side.
- The interaction circle is a pedestrian interaction zone immediately before the gate. Keep it outside the live vehicle lanes; provide a clearly marked pedestrian connection and a vehicle stopping/turning apron so cars can reach the ship approach without driving through the player interaction pad.
- Verify turning radii, approach gradients, collision continuity, guardrails, and the actual ship-gate position against the final ship mesh before fixing dimensions.

## Visual direction
- Preserve the approved luxurious gold-and-deep-blue Egyptian royal palette, illuminated bridge rails and lamps, royal banners, monumental ship gate, and warm golden lighting.
- The circle should be bright and clearly legible, with a subtle animated ring and a visible interaction prompt when a player is in range.
- Keep the road wide and visually readable, with clear lane markings and protected sidewalks.

## Explicit implementation boundaries
- This document records the user's approved visual concept only.
- It does **not** authorize moving the ship, changing production scenes, replacing approved buildings, or merging into `main`.
- No final 3D model, collision, vehicle handling, interaction code, Godot validation, or in-game render is claimed by this document.
- The generated image is a concept reference, not a screenshot of the actual game model. The actual model must be rendered from the same final scene/model before approval for integration.
