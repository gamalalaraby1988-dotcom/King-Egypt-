## Ground collision extents

The production ground is made from four collision-backed boxes. Their plan extents are:

| Ground node | X range (m) | Z range (m) | Collision |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Ground_West | -160 to -12 | -340 to 180 | Yes |
| Ground_East | 12 to 160 | -340 to 180 | Yes |
| Ground_Center_North | -12 to 12 | -340 to 78 | Yes |
| Ground_Center_South | -12 to 12 | 102 to 180 | Yes |

The existing production marina basin is only 24 × 24 m at (0, 90), matching the 24m gap between the center north/south ground sections. The expanded preview sea is 190 × 42 m at (0, 86), so it spans X=-95..95 and Z=65..107.

The expanded sea rectangle overlaps the existing collision-backed ground as follows:

| Ground node | Overlap X | Overlap Z | Overlap area |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Ground_West | 83 m | 42 m | 3,486 m² |
| Ground_East | 83 m | 42 m | 3,486 m² |

| Ground_Center_South | 24 m | 5 m | 120 m² |
| **Total** |  |  | **7,404 m²** |

This is a significant hidden-ground collision risk. If the preview's open-sea visual is copied into production without reshaping/removing the overlapping ground collision, players may continue walking on the ground beneath the deep-water visual. The water visual itself currently has no depth-boundary or player-blocking logic.

The shallow band (180 × 5 m, centered at Z=108) also overlays existing ground collision:
- West and east ground: 780 m² combined.
- Center-south ground: 120 m².
- Total shallow-band overlap: 900 m².

The sandy beach visual (180 × 46 m, centered at Z=130) also overlays existing ground collision:
- West and east ground: 7,176 m² combined.
- Center-south ground: 1,104 m².
- Total beach overlap: 8,280 m².

The existing ground collision top is approximately Y=-0.05 (ground center Y=-0.55, thickness 1m). The preview beach surface top is Y=0.17, so without a dedicated beach collision surface, a player may settle roughly 0.22m below the visible sand. The preview water surface top is approximately Y=0.03, only 0.08m above the existing ground collision top; that does not create a deep-water boundary.

## Existing ship and pier access elevations

The production ship is generated by `_build_royal_ship()` at world position (0, 0, 85). Relevant geometry:

| Element | World Y range / top |
|---|---:|
| Ship hull (center Y=1.0, height 1.8) | 0.10 to 1.90 |
| Main deck (center Y=2.12, height 0.28) | 1.98 to 2.26 |
| Side pier (center Y=0.02, height 0.28) | -0.12 to 0.16 |
| Option B arrival platform (center Y=0.55, height 0.55) | -0.275 to 0.825 |
| Option B bridge deck (center Y=0.52, height 0.45) | 0.295 to 0.745 |
| Option B bridge road (center Y=0.80, height 0.08) | 0.76 to 0.84 |
| Option B walkways (center Y=0.86, height 0.12) | 0.80 to 0.92 |
| Option B land ramp (center Y=0.30, height 0.30) | 0.15 to 0.45 |

The pier's top is Y=0.16 while the ship's main-deck top is Y=2.26: a vertical rise of about **2.10m**. The arrival platform top is Y=0.825, still about **1.44m below** the main-deck top. The platform and pier overlap in plan, but the existing geometry does not provide a safe, continuous boarding ramp or stair to the ship deck.

There are also vertical discontinuities in the current schematic bridge pieces:
- The bridge road top is Y=0.84, while the land-ramp top is Y=0.45.
- The bridge deck ends at Z=108 and the ramp begins at Z=108, but both are flat boxes with different top elevations; the ramp is not sloped.
- The arrival platform ends at Z=90.5 and the bridge begins at Z=91, leaving a 0.5m plan gap.
- The spur and bypass are near one another in plan, but their surfaces are separate flat boxes; a smooth vehicle transition is not established.

These are schematic blockout dimensions, not runtime-tested walkable profiles.

## Recommended next design before any production edit

1. Define one surveyed shoreline line and split the existing ground meshes/collisions to match it. Do not simply overlay the wide sea rectangle on the four existing ground boxes.
2. Give the beach its own visible sand surface and matching walkable collision at the intended top elevation.
3. Define shallow-water floor collision and a clear depth threshold; walking is permitted only in the shallow zone. Deep water must prevent continued ground walking and apply the intended boundary/recovery behavior.
4. Design a continuous bridge-to-platform transition with real sloped geometry, vehicle clearance, protected pedestrian paths, and no gaps.
5. Design a ship gangway or boarding ramp from the platform/pier elevation to the actual main-deck elevation, with handrails and collision. Keep the approved ship fixed.
6. Check all shoreline surfaces against the beach handball apron, beach gym, marina terminal, yacht club, and the complete southern road network.
7. Run Godot scene validation and player/vehicle collision tests before treating any of these surfaces as implemented.
